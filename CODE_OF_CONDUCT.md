# Code of conduct

English | [中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh.md)

## Our pledge

We want participation in AgentOps-DSH to be a good experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act in ways that make this project open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith, and assume the other person is acting in good faith too.
- Be respectful. Disagreement is expected; contempt is not.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully, and give it kindly.
- Focus on what is best for the community and for the people using the project.
- Show empathy towards other people and their situations.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any communication channel used to represent the project — and when an individual is officially representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through a private GitHub message or by opening a confidential issue through [private vulnerability reporting](https://docs.github.com/en/code-security/security-advisories/guidance-on-reporting-and-writing-information-about-vulnerabilities/privately-reporting-a-security-vulnerability), which reaches the same maintainers. All reports are reviewed, and the privacy and security of the reporter is respected.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by the rest of the maintainers.
